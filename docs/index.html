Tesla Remote

Control your Tesla from your Pebble. Free and open source.

Before you start

Setup takes about 10 minutes. You'll need:

  1. Your Tesla account email and password.
  2. Your phone's web browser (Chrome or Safari). Tesla's pages don't open inside this settings screen, so you'll copy links from here into your browser and copy a few things back.
  3. To be near your car for the last step.

This page remembers everything as you go, so it's fine to switch apps and come back. It's free: Tesla gives every account a monthly allowance that covers normal use.

1
Put your car key online

Your phone just made a private key for your car. Tesla needs to see the public half on a website. A free site called Fleet Key does that for you.

  1. Tap Copy key.
  2. Tap Copy Fleet Key link, open Chrome or Safari, paste it into the address bar and go.
  3. On Fleet Key, paste your key and create your address.
  4. Fleet Key shows an address that looks like abc12.fleetkey.net. Copy it, come back here, and paste it below.
Show my public key
2
Make your free Tesla app

Tesla asks everyone who connects to a car to register a small "app" on their developer site. It's free, and it's yours alone.

  1. Tap Copy Tesla link, paste it into your browser, and sign in with your Tesla account.
  2. Start a new application and fill in the form with these answers. Tap Copy next to an answer to copy it. Tesla's wording may differ a little.
  1. Finish and submit. Then open your new app's details and find its Client ID and Client Secret. Copy each one into the boxes below.
3
Sign in with Tesla
  1. Tap Copy sign-in link, paste it into your browser, and log in to Tesla. Your password only goes to Tesla.
  2. You'll land on a page that says Signed in to Tesla. Tap Copy code.
  3. Come back here, paste the code below, and tap Finish straight away. Codes expire after a few minutes.

This screen closes and your watch connects to Tesla. Then open these settings again for the last step.

4
Add the key to your car
  1. Go to your car with your phone.
  2. Tap Copy car link, paste it into your browser, and go. Your Tesla app opens and asks you to approve a new key. Approve it. The car may ask you to tap your key card on the console.
  3. Test it: on your watch, hold Select (or tap More), then choose Controls → Flash lights.

Once a command works, this step ticks itself off.
