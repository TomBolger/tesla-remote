Tesla Remote

Free, open-source Tesla control for Pebble. Everything runs on your phone.

1Your car key

This phone made a private key for your car. Only the public half below ever leaves your phone.

2Host the public key

Tesla needs to see your public key on a website. Fleet Key does this for free: open it, paste the key, tap Create, then copy the address it gives you (like abc12.fleetkey.net).

Open Fleet Key

3Create your Tesla developer app

On Tesla's developer site, choose Create Application. Use any name, then fill these in:

https://fleetkey.net

Scopes to tick: Vehicle Information, Vehicle Commands, Vehicle Charging Management. Tesla gives every account a monthly credit that covers normal personal use.

Open Tesla developer site

4Sign in with Tesla

You'll log in on Tesla's own page. Your password never touches this app.

5Add the key to your car

After you've signed in and saved, open this link on your phone. The Tesla app will ask you to approve the key. Do this near the car.
