Tesla Remote

Free, open-source Tesla control for Pebble. Everything runs on your phone.

1Your car key

This phone made a private key for your car. Only the public half below ever leaves your phone.

2Host the public key

Tesla needs to see your public key on a website. Fleet Key does this for free.

1. Tap Copy public key above.
2. Copy the Fleet Key link below and paste it into Safari or Chrome.
3. Paste your key there and tap Create.
4. Copy the address it shows (like abc12.fleetkey.net), come back here, and paste it below.

https://fleetkey.net

3Create your Tesla developer app

Copy this link into Safari or Chrome (or open it on a computer), sign in, and choose Create Application. Fill in Tesla's form using the cheat sheet below. Tesla may word things slightly differently.

https://developer.tesla.com/request

When Tesla shows your new app, copy its Client ID and Client Secret into the boxes below. Tesla gives every account a monthly credit that covers normal personal use, so you don't need to add billing details.

4Sign in with Tesla

Tesla's login doesn't work inside the Pebble window, so you'll use your normal browser:

1. Tap Copy sign-in link.
2. Paste it into Safari or Chrome and log in to Tesla.
3. You'll land on a page showing your sign-in code. Tap Copy code.
4. Come back here, paste it below, and tap Save to watch straight away (codes expire after a few minutes).

Or try signing in inside this window (works with some Pebble apps).

✓Signed in to Tesla

Your sign-in code:

Now go back to the Pebble app, open Tesla Remote settings, paste this into step 4, and tap Save to watch. The code expires in a few minutes.

5Add the key to your car

Do this after you've signed in and tapped Save to watch. Copy the link, paste it into Safari or Chrome on this phone, and the Tesla app will ask you to approve the key. Be near your car.
